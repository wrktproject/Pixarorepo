⚡ Quick Tips & Tricks

💡 Editing Order Matters:

Follow this sequence for best results:

  1. Crop and straighten
  2. Exposure and white balance
  3. Highlights and shadows
  4. Color adjustments (HSL, saturation)
  5. Detail work (sharpening, noise reduction)
  6. Creative effects (vignette, grain)
💡 Before/After Comparison:

Hold the Spacebar to temporarily view your original image. This helps you avoid over-editing!

💡 Start Subtle:

It's better to under-edit than over-edit. You can always go back and increase adjustments, but overly processed photos look unnatural.

💡 Use Presets as Starting Points:

Apply a preset you like, then fine-tune individual sliders. This speeds up your workflow while maintaining consistency.

💡 Save Your Best Edits as Presets:

When you create a look you love, save it as a custom preset. Build your own signature style library!

🎯 Photography Tips for Better Results

Great editing starts with great photos. Keep these tips in mind when shooting:

📷 Shoot in RAW

RAW files contain more data than JPEGs, giving you far more flexibility when editing exposure and colors.

🌅 Golden Hour

Shoot during the first hour after sunrise or the last hour before sunset for warm, flattering light.

📏 Rule of Thirds

Place subjects along imaginary gridlines that divide your frame into thirds for more dynamic compositions.

💡 Watch the Histogram

Check your camera's histogram to avoid blown highlights or crushed shadows that can't be recovered in editing.
